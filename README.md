# SwiftJ2K

JPEG 2000 and HTJ2K for the **Swift Image Compression Suite**.

**Status: Milestones 2 to 9 executed on the owner's assignment; a 12.1.0 release preparation passed every gate, and the owner deferred the release until the migration of J2KSwift is complete (JP3D, JPIP and CLI breadth remain; programme in [IMPLEMENTATION.md](IMPLEMENTATION.md)). No release exists.** The library encodes, decodes and inspects JPEG 2000 Part 1 (lossless, near-lossless and lossy) and HTJ2K Part 15 (lossless) codestreams and ISO/IEC 15444-1 `JP2`, ISO/IEC 15444-15 `JPH` and ISO/IEC 15444-2 single-codestream `JPX` containers (`jP  `, `ftyp`, `rreq`, `ihdr`, `bpcc`, `colr` enumerated and ICC profiles, `pclr` + `cmap` palette LUT expansion, `cdef` straight/premultiplied alpha and channel association, `res `/`resc`/`resd`, and `xml `, `uuid`, `uinf`/`ulst`/`url `, `jp2i`, `asoc`/`lbl ` ancillary metadata bounded by `ResourceLimits.maximumMetadataBytes`, `maximumICCBytes` and `maximumNestingDepth`) across unsigned and signed 1–16-bit integer samples in 8-bit or 16-bit storage, `1...N` components (greyscale, RGB planar/interleaved, RGBA and general multi-component), reversible 5/3 and irreversible 9/7 wavelets, Reversible Colour Transform (RCT) and Irreversible Colour Transform (ICT), scalar-derived (`Sqcd = 1`) and scalar-expounded (`Sqcd = 2`) dead-zone quantisation with midpoint reconstruction and PCRD-opt rate control (`quality`, `targetBitsPerPixel`, `qualityLayers`, `layerBitrates`, `.nearLossless(maximumAbsoluteError:)`), Region of Interest (`RGN` `0xFF5E` MaxShift), multi-tile, multi-layer, explicit precincts, all five progression orders (`LRCP`, `RLCP`, `RPCL`, `PCRL`, `CPRL`) plus `POC` (`0xFF5F`), `SOP`/`EPH`, all six Part 1 code-block style bits, extended guard bits (`1...7` with 64-bit internal bit-plane coding), and transcodes losslessly between `jpeg2000-codestream`, `htj2k-codestream`, `jp2`, `jph`, `jpx` and `jpx-htj2k` in any combination directly in the wavelet coefficient domain (`pixelAllocations == 0`, `copyEvents == 0`). Part 15 HT decoding covers cleanup-pass codestreams (`HTONLY`, `HTDECLARED` and `HTMIXED` per-block dispatch and placeholder passes; HT `SigProp`/`MagRef` refinement passes and multiple HT sets are rejected with `unsupportedFeature`). Hardware acceleration (`Backend.accelerated`: Metal GPU compute kernels and portable Swift SIMD/NEON vectorised kernels for 5/3 & 9/7 DWT, RCT/ICT colour transforms, dead-zone quantisation, MaxShift ROI scaling/descaling, and HTJ2K 2×2 quad classification and cleanup coding) is integrated behind `ExecutionPolicy` (`PLAT-05`, Decision M1) and reported in `OperationReport.backend` and `OperationReport.fallbackReason`, with 100% byte-identical output against `.scalarCPU`. The `swiftj2k-cli` executable encodes, decodes, inspects, validates and transcodes (`j2k`, `htj2k`, `jp2`, `jph`, `jpx`, `jpx-htj2k`) with `--backend auto|scalar|accelerated`. The intended first stable library version is **12.1.0**; it is not a published release, and contract 0.10.0's continuous-integration precondition is recorded per run. The package has no dependencies, and its capabilities advertise exactly the coverage above. Evidence: [MILESTONE2.md](Documentation/MILESTONE2.md), [MILESTONE3.md](Documentation/MILESTONE3.md), [MILESTONE4.md](Documentation/MILESTONE4.md), [MILESTONE5.md](Documentation/MILESTONE5.md), [MILESTONE6.md](Documentation/MILESTONE6.md), [MILESTONE7.md](Documentation/MILESTONE7.md), [MILESTONE8.md](Documentation/MILESTONE8.md), [MILESTONE9.md](Documentation/MILESTONE9.md); release procedure: [RELEASE.md](Documentation/RELEASE.md).

SwiftJ2K is the standalone successor to [J2KSwift](https://github.com/Raster-Lab/J2KSwift). The successor is intended to provide a harmonised API, explicit memory ownership, high-precision sample preservation and efficient shared-storage integration. It has no mandatory dependency on another suite library or CompressionFamily. Apache-2.0 licensing applies to these documents and subsequent authorised in-house implementation; third-party material retains its own terms.

## Native in-memory transcoding

Standalone **lossless J2K ↔ HTJ2K transcoding** in `Transcoder` and `swiftj2k-cli transcode` operates directly on decoded wavelet subband coefficients in memory (`pixelAllocations == 0`, `copyEvents == 0`), preserving every logical sample, declared precision, tile/origin geometry, per-tile decomposition level overrides and main/tile-part `COM` marker segments under `MetadataPolicy.preserve` (or omitting them under `.discardAncillary`, bounded by `ResourceLimits.maximumMetadataBytes`) across both directions. See [transcoding instructions and source-review findings](TRANSCODING.md) and [Milestone 6 evidence](Documentation/MILESTONE6.md).

Milestone 6 audit status, HT breadth reconciliation (`SigProp`/`MagRef` and multiple active HT sets remain `unsupportedFeature`) and qualification gates are tracked in [MILESTONE6.md](Documentation/MILESTONE6.md).

## Relationship to the suite

The four independent libraries are SwiftJ2K, SwiftJLS, SwiftJXL and SwiftJLI, all intended to live under Raster-Lab. A future optional umbrella adapts them for codec selection and in-process transcoding. The codecs do not depend on that umbrella. SwiftCompressionFamily is not part of this successor plan. The common contract is mirrored documentation plus behavioural tests, not a shared runtime package.

The main module is `SwiftJ2K`; `swiftj2k-cli` is the executable. A [compiled independent consumer](Examples/Consumer) and the [migration example](Examples/MigrationExample) demonstrate the current API. Features from the predecessor are migration candidates whose exact coverage must be verified; see IMPLEMENTATION.md. Nothing here changes the predecessor repository's current maintenance configuration.

## Command-line help and manual

The `swiftj2k-cli` executable encodes, decodes, inspects and validates lossless greyscale JPEG 2000 and HTJ2K codestreams through the contract's NRRD interchange profile, and transcodes losslessly between `j2k` and `htj2k` codestreams, with pipes, atomic output, JSON reports and the full exit-status map. Verbosity has five levels: `-v`, `-vv`, `--verbose 1..5`, `--verbose=+++` and `-verbose: 3`; diagnostics use stderr and `--quiet` suppresses optional messages. See [CLI usage and installation](CLI.md). The installer updates both the executable and its UNIX man page together. [OS 27/CLI qualification](Documentation/Engineering/OS27CLI/README.md) supersedes the earlier OS 26 upgrade decision; earlier evidence remains historical.
