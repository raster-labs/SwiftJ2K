No fixed tail-latency threshold was supplied for this task. Further event tracing would be needed for any stronger tail claim.

PERF-03 disposition to record
A narrow acceptance is defensible because the regression was measured repeatedly, the distinct transcode path was investigated, the added costs are now linked to required admission/compaction work, exact samples/bytes and resource invariants are preserved, and no new distinct accidental hotspot was found. Record candidate03 identities and the full observed cost range/spread rather than silently resetting thresholds. Keep the rejected67c8f7e predecessor labelled comparator only. Future work may reduce bookkeeping cost while preserving admission and overlap, but a globally optimal implementation or recovery of unsafe predecessor speed is not a prerequisite.

Scope remains two synthetic volumes/twelve operation cases on the recorded scalar hardware/toolchain. This recommendation does not establish full-corpus/R4/platform/concurrent performance, direct Part10+15, direct full38 Part2, multiple active HT sets, or overall R3 acceptance.
