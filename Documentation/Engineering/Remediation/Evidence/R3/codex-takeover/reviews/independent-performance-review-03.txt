SwiftJ2K R3 independent performance review 03 — candidate03 residual-cost decision
2026-10-31; /root/independent_core_review; read-only recommendation

Recommendation
Support an explicitly recorded, narrowly scoped safety/correctness trade-off for the measured encoder regressions, including the small EBCOT case. The new preallocation/live-overlap work is necessary to retain the repaired resource contract, its contribution is supported by the source and encoder profile, and the concrete invariant-string overhead identified in that profile has been removed. No further encoder optimisation is a prerequisite to that bounded decision; proving a globally optimal implementation or recovering the unsafe predecessor's speed is not required.

Do not yet fold odd8 HT-to-EBCOT into the same disposition. Its required added work has a credible source explanation, but the candidate's new wide timing spread is not explained by the medium16 HT-encode profile. One bounded diagnostic of the actual odd8 transcode path, rather than a speculative production change, is the remaining targeted investigation I recommend before closing all twelve measured cases. Root retains the final engineering decision. This report does not approve R3 overall.

Supported explanation and exception scope
Prior profile normalized to active encode frames found budget ancestry0.15%->5.85% and HT cleanup11.70%->19.24%. Source changes route real live reservations through encoder entropy/Tier2 work rather than admitting completed block data after allocation. The new ledger keeps old/replacement capacities, fusion/output overlap and concurrent retained output charged. Both entropy modes share this boundary; MQ additionally admits 64-byte growth and exact-output compaction. These are concrete safety operations, not a backend/fidelity reduction or additional full decoded-image handoff. The hoist changes only repeated invariant label construction; no limits, capacities, event labels, atomic admission or release ordering change.

This supports accepting the measured encoder cost as a deliberate bounded trade-off if root records the safety reason, exact candidate/baseline identities, absolute and percentage cost/spread, and a new validated successor baseline for these cases. It does not prove every nanosecond unavoidable; PERF-03 does not require that stronger claim. Keep the rejected predecessor labelled comparator only. A trade-off for these two volumes on Mac16,13/macOS27.0.1/Swift6.4 is not full-corpus, OS26, concurrency, R4 or release qualification.
