Evidence checked
I recomputed independent-median and paired latency ratios from all 480 run02 and 160 run03 samples, checked every JSONL sample against its archived stdout, checked the AB/BA order, and rehashed both benchmark binaries against their metadata. Every sample reports scalarCPU, one worker, five warmups, one timed iteration, exact samples, thermal state 0 and low-power mode false. Each case/version has 20 timed samples. The same binary and input identities are used across runs. The existing source review verified timing excludes input preparation and sample verification. Build receipts specify release mode. Hashes are recorded in independent-performance-review-01-source.json; all 52 scoped production sources and candidate-snapshot-01 still match review02.

Run02's encoded-size-audit.json binds its exact summary SHA256 and agrees with raw sizes. Run03 integrates the flag in summary.json. No separate run03 audit file is required.

Safety evidence and causal limits
The separately retained candidate-core-asan-01 receipt has exit0, unchanged inputs, and 44 passing tests across eight suites. Its native log hash matches the receipt. This strengthens the scoped admission/cancellation/sample regressions; it does not explain timing or close external qualification.

I inspected the proposed changed path: HT emitters add admitted 64-byte replacement transactions before reserveCapacity; reserveTransient/releaseScratch and heldScratch traverse the stated locks while old capacity remains charged. The code supports the implementer's hypothesis. It does not prove actual reallocation/copy counts, lock contention, or that six lock acquisitions per replacement are the minimum necessary for safety. One worker implies bookkeeping overhead may be uncontended. Do not call the entire measured increase an unavoidable cost of correctness yet.

What supports a deliberate safety trade-off
PERFORMANCE.md contract0.7.0 PERF-03 requires a recorded reason, measured cost and updated baseline; unexplained confirmed regressions block stable release. A defensible narrow decision would identify the repaired preallocation/live-overlap invariant and why baseline behaviour is unsafe, measure which transactions account for the increase, distinguish essential admission from avoidable implementation overhead, quantify remaining absolute and percentage costs with the repeated case/spread evidence, and record an explicit acceptance plus the new validated baseline and its limited scope. A causal profile or bounded diagnostic comparison can support attribution; instrumented timings must remain separate from uninstrumented acceptance measurements. A smaller RSS number or passing ASan does not supply this decision.

If profiling points to excess growth/locks/copies, first evaluate a narrow optimisation preserving admission before allocation, complete old/new overlap, atomic concurrent caps, denial/cleanup, cancellation, narrow-budget success behaviour, and telemetry ordering. Do not regain speed by reverting to delayed accounting, disabling budgets for one worker, or raising caps. Re-review and rerun relevant regressions plus controlled release comparisons after any change. PERF-03 allows an explicitly accepted, measured safety trade-off; it does not impose an absolute requirement to beat the unsafe baseline, nor authorise silent waiver because the work is safer.

Scope remains two synthetic volumes, twelve operation cases in run02 and four repeated encodes in run03, on macOS27.0.1/Apple Swift6.4/Mac16,13. This does not fulfil the full corpus, OS26, concurrent/per-platform, direct Part10+15, full38 Part2 peer or multiple-active-HT-set gates. No native process, source edit or acceptance action was performed by this reviewer.
