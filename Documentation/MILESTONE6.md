## Predecessor audit

Read at the pinned revision with `git show 7acc9ae4…:<path>` (the local J2KSwift working tree is not the pinned state). Executed 25 September 2026.

**Two HT implementations coexist in the predecessor; only one is Part 15.**

- The standard path is the `J2KHTConformant*` family in `Sources/J2KCodec`: `Tables` (the VLC source tables and 1024-entry lookups), `MELCoder`, `VLCCoder`, `MagSgnCoder`, `BitStream` (forward and reverse writers), `BlockLayout` (MagSgn ‖ MEL ‖ reversed VLC, Scup in the last 12 bits, Lcup/Scup split), `BlockEncoder` (its header states it is a port of OpenJPH's `ojph_encode_codeblock32`; pure-Swift loop `encodeLoopGeneric`), `BlockDecoder` (state machine with UVLC decoding, quad rows and sample reconstruction, a scalar twin of the SIMD path) and `Dispatch`. It implements the **cleanup pass only**: no HT SigProp or MagRef refinement passes, no multiple HT sets, no placeholder passes. The encoder always writes one pass and `zeroBitPlanes = K_max − 1`.
- `J2KHTBlockCoder.swift` (3,021 lines) and `J2KHTCodec.swift` are a **custom, non-Part-15 format** (a 6-byte per-block length header, a 2-bit significance-pattern VLC that is not the standard's, its own refinement framing). `J2KEncodingPresets.HTBlockFormat` defaults to `.conformant` and documents that `.custom` "is NOT decodable by OpenJPH". Under IMPLEMENTATION.md's rule this is legacy experimental material and does not migrate.
- The NEON C kernels (`Sources/J2KCodecNEON`) and the Metal HT stages are routed to by default but the Swift paths are self-contained once the routing branches, the Darwin-only profiling calls and the legacy-type extensions are removed.

**Signalling is inconsistent and the decoder never reads it.** The encoder writes Rsiz `0x4000`, CAP with `Pcap = 0x00020000` and a constant `Ccap15 = 0x0020`, CPF, COD with code-block style `0x40`, and a private COM marker `J2KSWIFT-HT:conformant`; `J2KHTCodec` and `J2KHTConformanceAPI` each use a different CAP/CPF convention, and the conformance API's validator would warn on the pipeline's own output. The decoder skips CAP and CPF entirely, detects HT from code-block style bit 6 only, ignores bit 7, misreads Scod bits 3–4 as a made-up "HT set" flag with an extra byte, and chooses between the conformant and custom block decoders by a heuristic on the first block's bytes when the private COM marker is absent, so any third-party codestream is at the mercy of that guess. Packet headers use Part 1 segment rules only; a block with more than one HT pass is not rejected. Decoded magnitudes are not scaled by 2^(K_max − 1 − missingMSBs), which is only correct for the predecessor's own output.

**The recorded lossless failure was an encoder bug, diagnosed and fixed.** Four of 49 CID22 images did not round-trip through `j2k --htj2k --lossless`; the conformant encoder derived K_max from single-level subband gains, multi-level 5/3 coefficients of high-contrast content overflowed into the sign bit (`comp=1 sub=LL K_max=8 maxAbs=259`, the 8×8 reproducer `htj2k_lossless_fail_min`). OpenJPH reproduced the loss from the predecessor's output and decoded its own streams exactly in the predecessor, isolating the encoder. v10.24.1 fixed it with `htConformantReversibleGain` (LL = B+1, detail bands = B+2, +1 under RCT) used for both the QCD exponents and the per-block shift, with a regression test. The successor derives K_max from the same rule and tests the reproducer.

**The conformance documents overstate.** `PART15_HTJ2K_CONFORMANCE.md` (February 2026) claims SigProp/MagRef/bypass compliance and lossless transcoding two months before the conformant coder existed; `HTJ2K_CONFORMANCE_REPORT.md` claims "100% conformance" of the custom codec and its own Known Limitations say no interoperability with other implementations was tested. The one substantiated claim is v10.24.1's: predecessor-encoded lossless HT decodes bit-exactly in OpenJPH 0.27, Grok 20.3 and Kakadu 8.4 on five medical fixtures, one direction only. No predecessor test decodes an OpenJPH- or Kakadu-encoded codestream.

**`J2KTranscoder.swift` is not a transcoder and nothing in it migrates.** It parses no packet headers (the whole tile's bytes are handed to every block), replaces any block decode failure with zeros, right-shifts subband sizes (wrong for odd sizes), re-encodes with the custom `HTJ2KEncoder`, concatenates block payloads without packet headers and reports `metadataPreserved: true` unconditionally; its tests assert non-empty output. The CLI's other transcode cases decode to pixels and re-encode. TRANSCODING.md's Milestone 2 audit stands.

**Licence and provenance.** The conformant block coder is an acknowledged port of OpenJPH (BSD-2-Clause) inside the MIT-licensed predecessor. Adapting it therefore brings BSD-2-Clause-derived code into this repository: permitted, and requires OpenJPH's copyright and licence text to be retained in the adapted files and `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` to be amended to disclose the BSD-2-Clause OpenJPH-derived code in the shipped products. **Owner decision resolved 26 October 2026: "Adapt the existing coder with its notices (recommended)."**

**What migrates, what is written new.**

| Adapted with provenance (owner decision approved 26 October 2026) | Written new against ISO/IEC 15444-15 and Part 1 |
| --- | --- |
| VLC/UVLC tables and lookup construction; MEL, VLC and MagSgn coders; forward/reverse bit streams; block layout (Lcup/Scup); cleanup-pass encoder loop and decoder state machine; the K_max rule of v10.24.1 | CAP (Pcap, Ccap15 with the reversible and MAGB fields) and CPF parsing and writing; code-block style bits 6 and 7 (HT, HT mixed); HT packet-header segment rules and pass accounting; magnitude scaling by 2^(K_max − 1 − missingMSBs); rejection of unsupported HT pass structures (SigProp/MagRef, multiple HT sets) with `unsupportedFeature` until implemented; the J2K ↔ HTJ2K coefficient transcoder over this repository's tier-2 and block decoders; CLI `transcode`; every test |

**Oracle triangle established before writing code.** `Scripts/generate-htj2k-fixtures.py` encodes the ten synthetic images with OpenJPH 0.30.1 (`-reversible true`, HT only) and Kakadu 8.4.1 (`Cmodes=HT`) in twelve variants and decodes every codestream with `ojph_expand`, `kdu_expand` and `opj_decompress`: 120 codestreams under `Tests/SwiftJ2KTests/Fixtures/HTJ2K`, 90 sample-exact in all three decoders. The other 30 are Kakadu-only by construction and are recorded as such in `manifest.json`: three-layer HT streams carry code-blocks with seven passes (several HT sets), which OpenJPH refuses ("supports 1 quality layer only") and OpenJPEG refuses ("more than 3 coding passes in an HT codeblock"); `Cmodes=HT|HTMIX` streams are read by Kakadu alone. Two tool findings: OpenJPH 0.30.1 with `-num_decomps 0` encodes 16-bit samples of value 0 as 32768 on five of the ten images (all three decoders agree with each other and disagree with the source), so that variant is excluded; and Kakadu accepts `BYPASS`, `CAUSAL` and `RESTART` alongside `HT` but emits identical bytes, so those flags have no HT-only fixture.

## Scope delivered

- **Licence and provenance (`M6-OPENJPH-PROVENANCE`, owner decision approved 26 October 2026).** Adapted the predecessor's conformant Part 15 block coder (`J2KHTConformant*` at `7acc9ae415e7d0bc7d441e0f0277d5e150bd19ca`), derived from OpenJPH (`Copyright (c) 2019-2024, Aous Naman`), into [Sources/SwiftJ2K/Codec/HTTables.swift](../Sources/SwiftJ2K/Codec/HTTables.swift) and [Sources/SwiftJ2K/Codec/HTBlockCoder.swift](../Sources/SwiftJ2K/Codec/HTBlockCoder.swift) with `SPDX-License-Identifier: Apache-2.0 AND BSD-2-Clause`, full provenance headers and OpenJPH's BSD-2-Clause licence text retained in both files, and [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) amended for the shipped products and the 120 HTJ2K fixtures. The predecessor's custom non-Part-15 HT coder (`J2KHTBlockCoder.swift` / `J2KHTCodec.swift`) and broken transcoder (`J2KTranscoder.swift`) were excluded.
- **Part 15 block coder (cleanup pass, scalar CPU).** `HTMELEncoder`, `HTMELDecoder`, `HTReverseBitEmitter`, `HTMagSgnEncoder`, `HTMagSgnDecoder`, `HTBlockLayout` (`MagSgn ‖ MEL ‖ reversed VLC` with `Lcup` up to `65535` and 12-bit `Scup` up to `4079`), `HTBlockEncoder.encodeCleanup` and `HTBlockDecoder.decodeCleanup` in [Sources/SwiftJ2K/Codec/HTBlockCoder.swift](../Sources/SwiftJ2K/Codec/HTBlockCoder.swift), backed by the static VLC/UVLC tables and 1024-entry lookup tables in [Sources/SwiftJ2K/Codec/HTTables.swift](../Sources/SwiftJ2K/Codec/HTTables.swift).
- **Part 15 marker signalling (`CAP`, `CPF`, `COD`, `COC`, `Rsiz`).** [Sources/SwiftJ2K/Codec/Codestream.swift](../Sources/SwiftJ2K/Codec/Codestream.swift) parses, validates and writes `CAP` (`0xFF50`, `Pcap = 0x0002_0000`, `Ccap15` `HTONLY`/`HTDECLARED`/`HTMIXED`, `SINGLEHT`/`MULTIHT`, `HTREV`/`HTIRV`, `MAGB` Table A.3 nonlinear decoded-bit-plane bound) and `CPF` (`0xFF59`), tracks code-block style bits 6 (`0x40`, HT) and 7 (`0x80`, HT mixed) across main-header `COD` and tile/component `COD`/`COC` overrides, and validates consistency between `CAP` and `COD`/`COC` styles.
- **Tier-2 HT packet header and pass accounting.** [Sources/SwiftJ2K/Codec/Tier2.swift](../Sources/SwiftJ2K/Codec/Tier2.swift) implements ITU-T T.814 | ISO/IEC 15444-15 Clause B.10.6 mixed-mode per-block `isHT` detection (`zeroBitPlanes` threshold when `htMixed` is active) and HT codeword segment parsing (`Lcup` for 1 cleanup pass; `Lcup` + `Lref` for 2 or 3 passes in a single HT set). [Sources/SwiftJ2K/Codec/LosslessPipeline.swift](../Sources/SwiftJ2K/Codec/LosslessPipeline.swift) accounts for placeholder passes before the HT cleanup pass (`(passes - 1) % 3 == 0` with empty `Lref == 0` segment, `effectiveMissingMSBs = zeroBitPlanes + (passes - 1) / 3`), scales decoded magnitudes by $2^{K_{\max} - 1 - \text{effectiveMissingMSBs}}$, decodes mixed Part 1 / HTJ2K codestreams (`Cmodes=HT|HTMIX`, dispatching per block to `HTBlockDecoder.decodeCleanup` or `BitPlaneCoder.decode`), and rejects HT refinement passes (`SigProp`/`MagRef` with non-zero `Lref`) and multiple HT sets with `CodecError(.unsupportedFeature, ...)`.
- **HTJ2K lossless encoding, inspection and decoding.** `Encoder` and `Decoder` in [Sources/SwiftJ2K/CodecAPI.swift](../Sources/SwiftJ2K/CodecAPI.swift) support both `jpeg2000-codestream` and `htj2k-codestream` (`CodecOptions.BlockCoding.part1` / `.htj2k`) for unsigned 1–16-bit single-component greyscale images using the reversible 5/3 wavelet and the `htConformantReversibleGain` $K_{\max}$ rule (`LL = B + 1`, detail subbands `= B + 2`).
- **Native compressed-to-compressed J2K ↔ HTJ2K transcoding.** `Transcoder` in [Sources/SwiftJ2K/Transcoding.swift](../Sources/SwiftJ2K/Transcoding.swift) and `ScalarLosslessCodec.transcode` in [Sources/SwiftJ2K/Codec/LosslessPipeline.swift](../Sources/SwiftJ2K/Codec/LosslessPipeline.swift) transcode `jpeg2000-codestream` ↔ `htj2k-codestream` in both directions directly in the wavelet subband coefficient domain (`decodeTileCoefficients` → target code-block re-blocking and encoding), with `0` intermediate pixel allocations and `0` copy events, preserving multi-tile grids, image/tile origins, per-tile `COD`/`QCD` decomposition level overrides, and main-header and tile-part `COM` (`0xFF64`) marker segments when `metadataPolicy == .preserve` (default). When `metadataPolicy == .discardAncillary`, `COM` marker segments are omitted without allocating comment slices and `OperationReport` records `discardedMetadataCount`, `discardedMetadataBytes` and `discardedMetadataKinds: ["COM"]` (`API-07`). Every transcode allocation stage (`input-copy`, `source-geometry`, `target-geometry`, `output-writer`, `decode-state`, `coefficient-plane`, incremental per-block `block-encoding`, `packet-writer`, `output-data`) is preflighted with exact `MemoryLayout<...>.stride` + array-header accounting and admitted against `ResourceLimits` (`maximumWorkspaceBytes`, `maximumMemoryBytes`, `maximumCompressedBytes`, `maximumMetadataBytes`) before allocation or buffer growth (`M6-AUD-006`). Requests that would require wavelet reconstruction (changing `decompositionLevels`) are rejected with `CodecError(.unsupportedFeature, ...)` to preserve pure coefficient-domain transcoding semantics.
- **CLI (`swiftj2k-cli`).** [Sources/SwiftJ2KCLI/main.swift](../Sources/SwiftJ2KCLI/main.swift), [CLI.md](../CLI.md) and [ManPages/swiftj2k-cli.1](../ManPages/swiftj2k-cli.1) support `--format htj2k` (`ht`, `jphc`, `.jphc`, `.htj2k`) across `encode`, `decode`, `inspect`, `validate` and `transcode`, including binary standard input/output (`-`), format-declaration verification against codestream markers (scanning past leading `COM` markers), `--code-block` re-blocking, `--metadata-policy preserve|discard-ancillary`, atomic file publication with `--overwrite`, JSON reports (`processingPath`, `peakWorkspaceBytes`, `pixelAllocations`, `copyEvents`, `discardedMetadataCount`, `discardedMetadataBytes`, `discardedMetadataKinds`), and cooperative `SIGINT` cancellation (`130`). No `metadataPreserved` field exists.

## Findings

1. **Predecessor `J2KHTConformantBlockLayout` rejected valid `Lcup > 4096` blocks.** The predecessor enforced `Lcup <= 4096` instead of the ISO/IEC 15444-15 Clause 7.3.2 codeword segment limit `Lcup <= 65535` (`Scup <= 4079`), causing high-entropy 64×64 16-bit code-blocks (`Lcup` around `4500–9000` bytes) to fail layout assembly and parsing. Fixed in `HTBlockLayout.assemble` and `HTBlockLayout.parse`.
2. **Predecessor `fuseMELAndVLC` corrupted bit 7 of the MEL stream on boundary-byte fusion.** When `fusePossible` and `vlcLastGT8F` were both true, the predecessor executed `fused &= 0x7F`, clobbering bit 7 of `melAligned` (the MSB of the MEL stream) and corrupting code-blocks where MEL and VLC shared a boundary byte after a `> 0x8F` VLC byte (reproduced on `16×16` and `32×8` blocks of `g16_64x64_random`). Fixed in `HTBlockLayout.fuseMELAndVLC` so fusion occurs only when `!(vlcLastGT8F && (fused & 0x7F) == 0x7F && (fused & 0x80) != 0)` without masking bit 7 of `melAligned`.
3. **Predecessor `HTMELDecoder.read()` omitted bit-7 unstuffing across 32-bit word boundaries.** When the final byte of a 4-byte MEL read was `0xFF` (`unstuff == true`), `bitsRead` was decremented to `31`, but the first byte of the next 4-byte word was masked with `0xFF` instead of `unstuff ? 0x7F : 0xFF`. Fixed in `HTMELDecoder.read()`.
4. **Fuzzing exposed unbounded decoded magnitude on corrupted HT bitstreams and 32-bit lifting synthesis overflow.** Mutation fuzzing (`FuzzHarness` seed `20261026`, iteration 1617 on mutated `g10_300x200_gradient.kdu_ht_layers.j2k`) produced a corrupted codestream where decoded `K_max` / UVLC magnitudes overflowed 32-bit wavelet synthesis during multi-level inverse 5/3 lifting. Fixed by bounding `kMax <= BitPlaneCoder.maximumMagnitudeBitPlanes` and `scaled < (UInt64(1) << kMax)` in `HTBlockDecoder.decodeCleanup` and guarding `Wavelet53.synthesise` with 64-bit integer arithmetic and `Int32` range validation (`CodecError(.malformedInput, ...)`).
5. **Kakadu multilayer HT placeholder passes.** Placeholder-pass accounting and mixed-mode per-block detection permit all 120 reference HT codestreams to decode sample-exactly, including all 20 `kdu_ht_layers`/`kdu_ht_layers_lrcp` cases. These fixtures do not establish support for non-empty refinement segments or multiple active HT sets. Synthetic signalling-rejection tests cover those unsupported cases.
6. **Codex provisional static audit findings (`M6-AUD-001` and `M6-AUD-002`) resolved.**
   - `M6-AUD-001`: `ScalarLosslessCodec.transcode` previously computed progress `total = profile.codeBlockCount * 2`, which threw `CodecError(.invalidArgument)` inside `ProgressUpdate` when `--code-block` re-blocked a source into smaller target blocks (`completed > total`). Fixed by computing `total = profile.codeBlockCount + totalTargetBlocks` from the target tile geometries; verified by a `64×64` (1 block) → `4×4` (256 blocks) regression test asserting identical output with and without a progress callback.
   - `M6-AUD-002`: `ScalarLosslessCodec.transcode` previously fell back to inverse + forward 5/3 DWT when `codecOptions.decompositionLevels` differed from the source while capabilities still advertised only the coefficient path. Fixed by rejecting decomposition-level changes during transcode with `CodecError(.unsupportedFeature, ...)` while preserving per-tile `COD`/`QCD` decomposition-level overrides in tile-part headers on the pure coefficient path; verified by a multi-tile differing-level transcode test and CLI exit-4 check.
7. **Codex submission and delta audit findings (`M6-AUD-004`, `M6-AUD-005`, `M6-AUD-006`) resolved.**
   - `M6-AUD-004` (`COM` metadata policy + `API-07` discard reporting): `CodestreamSyntax.parse(_:limits:metadataPolicy:)` now validates `COM` (`0xFF64`) marker segments (`Lcom >= 4`) and checks `limits.maximumMetadataBytes` before allocating any slice; under `.preserve` (default), main-header and tile-part `COM` segments are preserved in both `J2K → HTJ2K` and `HTJ2K → J2K` directions; under `.discardAncillary`, `COM` segments are omitted without allocating comment slices and `OperationReport` (and `swiftj2k-cli --json` stderr reports) record `discardedMetadataCount`, `discardedMetadataBytes`, and `discardedMetadataKinds: ["COM"]`. Verified by `transcodePreservesAndDiscardsCOMMetadataAndEnforcesMetadataLimits` and 4 CLI checks in `Scripts/test-cli.py`.
   - `M6-AUD-005` (CI `independent-consumer` checkout path): `.github/workflows/contract.yml` checked out the repository into `path: package` while `Consumer/Package.swift` declared `package: "SwiftJ2K"`. Fixed by checking out into `path: SwiftJ2K` in `.github/workflows/contract.yml`.
   - `M6-AUD-006` (Pre-allocation resource admission and exact nested geometry/packet state accounting): Added `Tier2.preflightTileGeometry` using exact `MemoryLayout<...>.stride` and `32`-byte Swift array header accounting across `TileGeometry`, `CodeBlockContribution`/`PrecinctTrees`/`TagTree` decode state, and `CodeBlockEncoding`/`TagTree` encode state; admitted duplicate input `[UInt8](data)` before copying in `Transcoder.transcode`; admitted source and target geometries before `Tier2.makeTileGeometry`; admitted per-block retained encodings incrementally inside `encodeTileBlocks`; used a bounded `ByteWriter(reserving:maximumBytes:)` with `write*Checked` for header and packet assembly while releasing `plane = []`, `encodings = []`, and `bytes = []` before subsequent stage allocations; and verified pre-allocation denial using stage-labelled `StorageTelemetry.Recorder.events` in `transcodeResourceAccountingAndTelemetryCoverTargetGeometryEncodingsAndOutput`.
